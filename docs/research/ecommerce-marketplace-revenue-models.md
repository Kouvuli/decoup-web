# E-commerce marketplace revenue models

Verified 2026-09-08 from first-party pages. Exact rates and programs change; this note compares revenue mechanisms rather than recommending copied pricing.

## Decision update — 2026-09-10

The canonical specs retain Seller completion commission for managed marketplace Orders but choose Free/Pro subscriptions for service lead generation because service contracting and payment occur outside DecoUp. This supersedes the service-provider portion of the launch recommendation below; the marketplace comparison remains research evidence.

## Shopee Vietnam

Shopee's published terms describe seller-paid fees deducted from successful orders, including a payment fee and a category-dependent fixed commission. Shopee also offers seller visibility tools that improve product ranking, plus broader paid service packages. The durable pattern is transaction revenue plus optional seller growth tools.

Sources: [Shopee service terms](https://help.shopee.vn/portal/4/article/77243-%C4%90I%E1%BB%80U-KHO%E1%BA%A2N-D%E1%BB%8ACH-V%E1%BB%A4), [seller product boosting](https://help.shopee.vn/portal/1/article/97725-H%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Bng-d%E1%BA%ABn-s%E1%BB%AD-d%E1%BB%A5ng-t%C3%ADnh-n%C4%83ng-%C4%90%E1%BA%A9y-s%E1%BA%A3n-ph%E1%BA%A9m-t%E1%BB%B1-%C4%91%E1%BB%99ng), [seller service packages](https://help.shopee.vn/portal/4/article/124168-G%C3%93I-GI%E1%BA%A2I-PH%C3%81P-HI%E1%BB%82N-TH%E1%BB%8A-T%E1%BB%94NG-TH%E1%BB%82).

## Lazada Vietnam

Lazada's seller registration advertises temporary new-seller waivers for platform commission and provides advertising credit. Those promotions are direct evidence that platform commission and seller advertising are standard monetizable products outside the waiver. Lazada also operates a marketplace for seller services and an affiliate program, although affiliate commission is an acquisition expense paid by Lazada rather than direct marketplace revenue.

Sources: [Lazada Seller Center registration and incentives](https://sellercenter.lazada.vn/apps/register/index?gsc=1), [seller service marketplace](https://pages.lazada.vn/wow/i/vn/smp/landing), [affiliate program](https://pages.lazada.vn/wow/i/vn/corp/affiliate-howitworks?hybrid=1).

## Chợ Tốt

Chợ Tốt monetizes seller visibility and access directly: paid posting in selected categories, featured listings, priority/VIP placement, listing boosts, storefronts, and PRO packages. Its electronics PRO package bundles listing capacity and boosting for professional sellers. This model fits classifieds, where many transactions may complete outside platform checkout and therefore cannot reliably fund a transaction commission.

Sources: [paid and featured listing bundle](https://trogiup.chotot.com/nguoi-ban/goi-dang-tin-noi-bat/), [paid seller services catalog](https://trogiup.chotot.com/toi-la-nguoi-ban/goi-pro/), [electronics PRO package](https://trogiup.chotot.com/nguoi-ban/goi-pro-do-dien-tu-la-gi/), [storefront products](https://trogiup.chotot.com/toi-la-nguoi-ban/cua-hang-chuyen-trang/).

## Etsy

Etsy combines a listing fee, transaction fee, payment-processing fee, optional on-platform ads, and sale-attributed offsite advertising fees. This diversifies revenue but increases seller fee complexity. Its model is useful as a catalogue of mechanisms, not as a sensible launch bundle for DecoUp.

Sources: [Etsy fee basics](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360035902374-Etsy-Fee-Basics), [Etsy Offsite Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work).

## Mechanisms compared

| Mechanism | Charged when | Strength | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Completion commission | Order or Booking succeeds | Aligns revenue with seller/provider success | Requires reliable in-platform payment, completion, refunds and disputes |
| Payment-processing fee | Payment is processed | Recovers provider cost | Confusing if mixed into platform commission |
| Listing fee | Listing is posted or renewed | Works even when transactions leave the platform | Discourages early supply and casual used-item sellers |
| Promoted listing | Seller/provider buys extra visibility | Optional and understandable | Damages trust if labels or organic ranking are unclear |
| Professional subscription | Business buys recurring tools/capacity | Predictable revenue and good business segmentation | Premature before valuable professional tools exist |
| Third-party display advertising | Advertiser buys audience exposure | Revenue independent of transactions | Requires audience scale and adds privacy, UX, moderation and brand-safety costs |
| Offsite performance advertising | Platform advertises seller externally and charges attributed sales | Seller pays for outcomes | Attribution disputes and unexpectedly high effective fees |

## Recommendation for DecoUp

Launch with one disclosed completion commission paid by Sellers and Service Providers. Keep basic Listings and Service Offerings free so Ho Chi Minh City supply can grow. Add optional, clearly labeled promoted placement only after organic ranking and basic marketplace liquidity work.

Do not launch generic third-party display ads, mandatory listing fees, subscriptions, offsite attribution fees, or multiple overlapping service charges. Consider professional subscriptions only when verified businesses repeatedly use genuinely valuable tools. Keep payment-provider and shipping charges transparent and distinct from DecoUp's commission.

## Evidence limits

Public sources show fee categories and seller-facing offers, not full platform revenue composition or profit margins. Lazada's public seller page proves commission waivers and advertising credits but does not expose every current Vietnam seller fee. Before setting DecoUp percentages, separately model payment costs, refunds, support/dispute work, taxes, promotions, fraud loss and seller acquisition; competitor percentages alone are not a pricing strategy.
