# Service marketplace provider pricing and circumvention

**Status:** Product research\
**Date:** 2026-09-08\
**Scope:** Pricing presentation, provider monetization, and off-platform leakage for DecoUp services.

## Decision update — 2026-09-10

The canonical service specs now choose a lead-generation model: Free and Pro Provider plans, a three-month Pro trial, accepted-lead contact exchange, and external service contracting/payment. This supersedes the launch recommendation below to use completion commission; the comparative findings remain research evidence.

## Findings

### Service prices and custom quotes can coexist

- Fiverr supports clearly scoped packages and custom offers for requests outside those packages. Some categories also enforce minimum scope and pricing to keep offers realistic and comparable.
- DecoUp should not reuse the marketplace's **Price Offer** mechanic for services. A Service Offering should use one of: a fixed package, an honest starting price with a defined minimum scope, an assumption-based range, or **quote required**.
- A selected Service Provider responds to a Service Request with a Quote. The accepted Quote—not an artificially low profile price—is the Booking value and commission basis.

### Providers can pay completion commission

- Upwork charges freelancers a disclosed contract service fee and ties that fee to payment protection, fraud prevention, and dispute resolution.
- Fiverr pays freelancers 80% of completed order value, including extras.
- Taskrabbit demonstrates the alternative of charging customer-facing service and trust fees. This exists, but it does not mean DecoUp needs a customer fee at launch.
- A provider commission is therefore a normal marketplace model. DecoUp does not need to charge Buyers or Customers merely because Service Providers are not Sellers.

### Subscriptions do not solve bait pricing or leakage

- Fiverr's paid Seller Plus tiers provide analytics, marketing, support, and payout benefits. They are optional growth tools layered over transaction fees.
- Thumbtack advertises free provider signup without subscription or membership fees and lets providers control lead budgets.
- A paid provider plan can monetize tools or visibility later, but it does not prove that a displayed price is genuine and may let low-quality providers pay to remain visible.
- Launching Free, Pro, and Premium before providers demonstrate repeated needs would add policy and entitlement complexity without validating value.

### Prevent leakage with value plus proportionate rules

- Upwork and Fiverr prohibit off-platform payment and explain that users lose payment protection, disputes, support, and verified work history when they leave the platform.
- Upwork also restricts pre-contract contact sharing and reports detecting attempts to redirect payment off-platform.
- DecoUp cannot completely prevent participants from moving to Zalo. It should make on-platform Booking valuable through deposits or protected payment, Quote and Change Proposal records, dispute support, verified reviews, and provider work history.
- A balanced launch policy is to keep scoping chat and media in DecoUp, restrict obvious pre-Booking contact/payment redirection, then allow practical contact exchange after a Booking or deposit for onsite coordination. Payment and scope changes remain protected only when recorded in DecoUp.

## Recommendation for launch

1. One free Service Provider profile; no Free/Pro/Premium entitlement system yet.
2. Commission on successfully completed Bookings; no Customer platform fee at launch.
3. Service Offering prices must be fixed, a truthful starting price, an assumption-based range, or quote required.
4. Snapshot the displayed pricing basis when a Service Request is sent. A Quote outside that basis requires an explanation.
5. Monitor quote accuracy, cancellations, complaints, and repeated bait pricing; warn, demote, remove, or suspend repeat offenders.
6. Keep pre-Booking scoping in DecoUp. Allow contact exchange after commitment for onsite coordination, while limiting protection and verified reviews to on-platform completion.
7. Revisit a Pro plan only after providers repeatedly request concrete paid tools such as analytics, team scheduling, faster payout, or promoted placement. Do not create Premium until Pro is validated.
